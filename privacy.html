Last updated November 10, 2026

Jean Ropke operates Jean Ropke Maps. This policy covers the map directory at jeanropke.net and the map projects that link to this policy, including projects hosted at jeanropke.github.io. Features and third-party services can vary by project.

Hosting and map resources

When you open a map, your browser requests pages, map tiles, and other files from our hosting and content delivery providers. Those providers receive technical information such as your IP address and the files requested to deliver content, maintain their services, and protect them from abuse.

GitHub Pages records and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Map settings and progress

Depending on the map, your browser can store settings such as language, filters, map markers, and collectible progress so they are available on your next visit. Browser storage is specific to the browser and site. Settings and progress saved there can stay until you remove them, the browser clears site data, or the map replaces them.

Advertising

Some maps use Google AdSense to show and measure ads. When your browser requests ads, it can share information such as the page URL, IP address, and browser or device identifiers with Google and participating advertising providers. These providers may set or read cookies and use web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud.

Google and other advertising providers can use cookies to select personalized ads based on your previous visits to these maps or other websites. Ad personalization depends on your location, your choices, and the advertising settings that apply to you.

Where the map’s privacy message lists participating ad partners, you can use it to find their names and privacy links. Google describes its use and retention of advertising data in these resources.

  • How Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services
  • Google’s advertising and data retention information

Your privacy choices

  • Use the privacy controls on the map you visited to make choices for that site. The controls and options shown depend on your region.
  • Use Google My Ad Center to turn off personalized Google ads. You can also manage personalized ads from some participating advertising companies through YourAdChoices.
  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies and clear local site data.

EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

When the site asks for your consent, you can choose whether to allow the advertising purposes listed in the message. To change or withdraw consent later, open Privacy and cookie settings on the map you visited, where the control is available. Withdrawal applies going forward.

Technical requests are needed to deliver the maps you open. Where applicable, our legitimate interests include keeping the maps available and secure, preventing abuse, and responding to requests. We rely on consent for optional advertising storage and personalization where required. Hosting and advertising providers may process data outside your country. See the provider notices above for their processing locations, safeguards and retention practices.

Depending on applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, or object to certain processing. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

California and other U.S. states

The information described above can include identifiers such as IP addresses and cookie IDs, and internet activity such as pages viewed and interactions with ads. It comes from your browser and your use of the maps. Hosting and content delivery providers receive technical request data. Google and participating ad providers receive advertising data for the purposes described under Advertising.

Depending on the advertising settings in use, disclosures for advertising based on your activity across websites may be considered a sale or sharing of personal information or targeted advertising under applicable state law.

To opt out of the advertising disclosures covered by the AdSense control, open Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information on the map you visited, where available, and select Opt out. You can also enable Global Privacy Control (GPC) in a supported browser or extension. For eligible ad requests in applicable states, Google receives the GPC signal and applies restricted data processing.

Where applicable, state law may also give you rights to know or access the personal information collected about you, correct or delete it, obtain a copy, or limit certain uses of sensitive personal information. You have the right to exercise applicable privacy rights without discrimination.
